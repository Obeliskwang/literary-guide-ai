Story 1: The Birth of the Stone Monkey and King of Water Curtain Cave
On the Eastern Divine Continent stands the Mountain of Flowers and Fruit. At its summit lies an immortal stone that has absorbed the essence of the sun and moon for thousands of years. One day, the stone cracks open and produces a stone egg. When the wind blows across it, the egg turns into a stone monkey.
The monkey is agile and athletic. He plays with a troop of ordinary monkeys every day. One sweltering summer, the monkeys search for a cool stream to escape the heat. They spot a soaring waterfall hanging from a cliff. The monkeys make a bet: whoever dares to leap through the waterfall and find its source will be their king.
